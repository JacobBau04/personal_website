## Problem Statement

Design a compact, high-current board that receives commands and actuates 3 motors, incorporating a microcontroller, a switch, and motor drivers on a 2.5 cm × 2.5 cm board, while maintaining good PCB design practice: proper clearances, short traces, capacitors near their pins, and wiring according to the datasheets.

## Tasks

1. Designed a compact PCB
2. Soldered the components

## Result

An operational PCB, deployed in the PuzzleBots swarm robots.
