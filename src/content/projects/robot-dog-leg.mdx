## Overview

A 3-degree-of-freedom (DOF) robot leg that can move to any point in 3D space. It uses a custom nonlinear system-of-equations solver for the inverse kinematics equations.

<Figure src="robot-dog-leg/leg-photo.jpg" caption="The assembled leg" />

## My Role

- Mechanical and circuit design
- Wrote a custom nonlinear system-of-equations solver (Arduino didn't have a sufficient one)

<Figure src="robot-dog-leg/leg-cad.jpg" caption="CAD model of the leg" />

<Figure src="robot-dog-leg/solver-code.jpg" caption="Part of the custom nonlinear solver" />

## Result

A functional leg. The end effector moves in a straight line, with positions found by the custom nonlinear solver.
