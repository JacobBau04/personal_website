## Overview

An autonomous tenth-scale race car built in the Autonomous Car Racing (F1TENTH) course at the University of Pennsylvania, where I am the control and perception lead.

## What I Built

- **Perception:** built a LiDAR map via SLAM; implemented particle-filter localization on the autonomous race car
- **Control:** developing an LQR path-tracking controller; implemented pure pursuit, follow-the-gap, and PID wall following
- **Integration:** designed the ROS 2 software architecture integrating an NVIDIA Jetson, the motor controller, and a 2D LiDAR
