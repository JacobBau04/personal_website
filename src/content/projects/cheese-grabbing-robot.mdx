## Problem Statement

Design an autonomous robot that can reliably navigate a constrained competition arena to locate, collect, and return high-value objects within strict time, size, and sensor limits.

<Figure src="cheese-grabbing-robot/robot.jpg" caption="The robot and its claw mechanism" width={480} />

## Result

**2nd place out of 7 robots**

## Components

1. Task and motion planning
2. Claw mechanism design
3. Line-following routine
