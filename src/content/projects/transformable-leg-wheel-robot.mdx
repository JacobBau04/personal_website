## Overview

A transformable leg-wheel robot that adapts its gait to the environment in real time, using a reinforcement learning (RL) policy.

## My Contribution

- Developed 95% of the RL policy
- Developed all of the ROS 2 software
- Developed the simulation model
- Exported the policy to the physical robot

### Technical Details

- Developing a **reinforcement learning policy** that adapts leg extension and wheel torques to the terrain, using a base policy with an online adaptation module
- Built a GPU-accelerated MuJoCo simulation of the robot and terrain to train RL control policies
- Deploying the policy on hardware, using domain randomization and system identification to close the sim-to-real gap

## Results

- Energy-efficient locomotion in varied environments in simulation (see videos)
- Real-world locomotion with ROS 2
- Currently implementing the policy on the physical robot
- First-author paper in preparation for the IROS 2027 conference
