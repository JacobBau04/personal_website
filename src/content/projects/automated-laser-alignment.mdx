## Problem

Manual guide-star laser beam alignment required expert, hands-on tuning, and often sent two PhDs from our group on a two-hour drive to realign it when it drifted.

## Overview

Built an automated system that can measure and correct large misalignments, and deployed it on an operational research telescope.

<Figure src="automated-laser-alignment/bench-setup.jpg" caption="The alignment setup: camera, flip lens, state estimation and correction mirrors, and laser source" />

## What I Built

- Designed and deployed an **automated laser alignment system** on an operational research telescope
- Built a control pipeline with a proportional **closed-loop controller** and a **self-calibrating** control matrix
- **Designed and validated state estimation algorithms** using **custom simulation**, and benchmarked them against statistically ideal performance
- Inferred the system's state using non-standard methods based on wave optics theory

<Figure src="automated-laser-alignment/pointing-centering.jpg" caption="Inferring pointing and centering states from camera images, with and without alignment error" />

## Results

- Functional system and user manual
- The system is **still used by engineers** at LLNL for remote alignment
