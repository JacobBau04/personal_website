## Problem

Manual guide-star laser beam alignment required expert, hands-on tuning, and often sent two PhDs from our group on a two-hour drive to realign it when it drifted.

## Overview

Built an automated system that can measure and correct large misalignments, inferring the system's state using non-standard methods based on wave optics theory.
